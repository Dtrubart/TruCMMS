# Native maintenance reports

TruCMMS Work Register shows authorized orders with All, Open, Overdue, Technical Completed, Accepted, Closed, Cancelled and Unscheduled views. Target Closure is optional and frozen after authorization. Orders without a target are unscheduled rather than overdue.

TruCMMS Equipment History links authorized requests, work orders, executions, lifecycle events and execution adjustments. Access follows the source documents and organizational permissions. Export remains subject to native ERPNext permissions.

Supervisors can void an erroneous execution while its order is Assigned, with a required reason. The original remains immutable. Effective time excludes voided entries; a replacement can be recorded by the assigned technician. Completion requires an effective execution for the current cycle.

The synthetic multi-sector pilot is separate from the installable app. Preventive scheduling, specialized calibration and commercial contractor management are outside this release.
